# kiruthi--ma-

Forever Yours 🤍 — A Romantic Birthday Surprise Website

A beautiful, interactive birthday surprise web application made with love.

## ✨ Features
- 🩵 **Dreamy Sky Blue Aesthetic**: Starry night twilight galaxy theme with soft glowing celestial particles.
- 🤍 **White Glowing Hearts**: Pure white glowing hearts throughout the experience.
- 🎂 **Interactive Birthday Cake**: Candles you can blow out and make a wish with confetti bursts.
- 📸 **Our Memories Gallery**: Polaroid photos with tap-to-enlarge lightbox modal, touch swipe gestures, and love reactions.
- 💌 **Love Letter Envelope**: Interactive sealed envelope that unfolds and types out a heartfelt message.
- 🩵 **Reasons Why I Love You**: Heart card that reveals reasons with tap animations.
- 🎵 **Background Music Autoplay**: Continuous looping romantic song with a floating glowing soundwave controller.
- ♾️ **Infinity & Grand Finale**: Luminous infinity hearts and celebratory fireworks show.

## 🚀 How to Run
Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser on your phone, tablet, or desktop!
